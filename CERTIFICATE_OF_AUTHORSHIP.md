## What this declaration covers

1. The source code: `nirni_livewebcam.py`, `studio.py`, `air_canvas.py`, `gestures.py`,
   `tracking.py`, `effects.py`, `media.py`, `ui.py`, the tests, and the launcher scripts.
2. The gesture design of the program:
   - touching the nose tip to vanish into a saved background and touching it again to return,
   - four vanish styles (dissolve, pixel, portal, glitch),
   - touching the forehead to switch on draw tools, and the chin to switch on the effect window,
   - joining the index fingertips of both hands to draw in the air,
   - turning the stroke into a clean shape,
   - closing a hand like a fist over a shape to catch it and carry it, and closing a second
     fist to resize and rotate it,
   - the pointing-and-waiting tool buttons (colours, rainbow, eraser, undo, redo, effects,
     ghost, clone, photo, record),
   - the effect window between the index fingertips of two hands (blur, pixelate, magnifier),
   - ghost trail and shadow clones,
   - wink to undo and a wide smile for confetti,
   - photo and video capture of the clean output.
3. The documentation, the name "Nirni Livewebcam", and the project layout.

## What it does not cover
